# Insurance agents' AI frustrations + WhatsApp message for Patkan

## Goal
A short, human-sounding WhatsApp message to share with a network of insurance agents, built on the top 3 frustrations insurance agents have with ChatGPT/Gemini. No site or code changes — you copy the approved text and send it yourself.

## The top 3 frustrations (basis of the message)

1. **It makes things up — confidently.** Ask about a policy's features, exclusions, waiting periods or tax treatment and it can state numbers that exist in no brochure. In insurance one wrong number in front of a client costs credibility and can invite compliance trouble.
2. **The answer is wrong-shaped and wrong-place.** Either a two-page essay when the agent wanted three lines for a WhatsApp reply, or advice written for the US market — US tax codes, US insurers — instead of Indian products, IRDAI context and Indian clients.
3. **Getting a usable answer demands a long, structured prompt.** "Add role, context, format, constraints" is prompt-engineering advice for techies. An agent wants to type a rough thought and go, not learn a new skill.
