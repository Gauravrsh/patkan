# Ganesh Chaturthi LinkedIn artwork — text wireframe

## Locked direction

- **Canvas:** 1080 × 1350 px portrait.
- **Background:** Patkan warm cream (`#F3EFE4`).
- **Illustration:** one continuous, minimal Lord Ganesha silhouette in Patkan orange (`#D96C2C`).
- **Greeting:** `गणेश चतुर्थीच्या हार्दिक शुभेच्छा` in dark Patkan ink (`#1C1917`).
- **Footer:** quiet full-width band containing only the existing Patkan logo and `patkan.in`.
- **Style:** ceremonial, respectful, extremely minimal; no motifs, gradients, shadows, confetti, flowers, diyas, decorative borders, or extra copy.

## Proportions and placement

- Ganesha occupies approximately **34% of canvas width**, centered slightly above the visual midpoint.
- Greeting sits below it with generous breathing room, on **one centered line if naturally legible**, otherwise two balanced lines without shrinking excessively.
- Footer occupies approximately **11% of canvas height** and uses the same cream family with a fine ink divider, not a contrasting block.
- Logo appears at left; `patkan.in` aligns right on the same optical baseline.
- Wide safe margins keep all important content clear of LinkedIn cropping.

## After wireframe approval

1. Create the final artwork at exactly 1080 × 1350 px using the established Patkan palette and current logo.
2. Use a clean Devanagari-capable typeface that visually harmonizes with Patkan’s typography.
3. Inspect the final image at native size and LinkedIn feed size for line clarity, respectful silhouette readability, Marathi text rendering, balance, and footer legibility.
4. Deliver a high-resolution PNG in the Files section.
