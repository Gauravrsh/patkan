# Patkan Terms of Use v1.0: full draft for your approval

This is a draft for your review only. Nothing gets built until you approve. After approval I will add it as a `/terms` page, link it in every footer and from the privacy policy, and add metadata. No other code changes.

Important caveat: a Terms document lowers your risk, but it cannot fully protect you. As a sole owner in India, your personal assets and the business are legally the same thing. Indian law also does not allow a contract to exclude liability for fraud, gross negligence or statutory consumer rights (Indian Contract Act s.23, Consumer Protection Act 2019). The strongest protection is structural: (1) register a One Person Company or LLP and make it the operator named in these Terms and the policy, and (2) have a licensed Indian advocate review the final text. The draft below is written so that swapping "Gaurav Sharma" for a company name later is a one-line change.

## What we took from WinRAR, iLovePDF and similar free products

- **WinRAR EULA:** the software is supplied "as is". The user accepts all risk of using it. The author is not liable for any damages, including lost data or lost profit. The licence is short, blunt and survives in every jurisdiction because it promises nothing. Used in sections 9, 10 and 11.
- **iLovePDF Terms:** the user is fully responsible for the files and content they process. Content is processed automatically, with no human review. The service can change, pause or end at any time. Liability is capped at the amount the user paid, which for free users is effectively zero. One governing law and venue apply. Used in sections 5, 6, 8, 11 and 15.
- **Common to all of them:** a free service with no service-level promise, the right to rate-limit and block users, a severability clause, and a clause saying continued use means acceptance of changes. All of these are included.

## Placeholders you must confirm

- `[CITY]` for the courts where disputes are heard. Recommended: your city of residence, for example New Delhi or Bengaluru.
